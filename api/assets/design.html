01 · Depth — the law of the three layers

One fake light source, top-left. Highlights fall up-left, shadows down-right. bg is the page. mg is the same flesh, lifted by shadow alone. fg sits on mg and casts onto it. Inputs are dents — you type deeper. Buttons rise toward the cursor — the cursor is the highest point of the world.

bg — the page itself

The ground everything rests on. No shadow of its own.

mg

Cards, panels, sections. Same color as bg — shadow is the only difference.

Buttons, knobs, badges. Cast onto mg; rise toward the cursor on hover.

Inputs press down — inset shadows make a dent you type into.
